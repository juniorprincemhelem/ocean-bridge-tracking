    HELP & SUPPORT

    Common shipment questions.

    For help with a specific shipment, include its tracking number in your message.

    Why hasn't my shipment updated?

    Tracking changes when a shipment event is recorded. Updates may pause while a parcel is moving between facilities or awaiting its next scan. If the pause seems unusual, send support your tracking number and the last update you can see.

    What should I do if customs is holding my shipment?

    Check any official notice for the documents or information requested. Customs decisions and charges are set by the relevant authorities and depend on the route and shipment. Contact our team with your tracking number before acting on an unexpected payment request.

    What if delivery is late or unsuccessful?

    Send us your tracking number and a short description of the issue. Our team can review the latest recorded event and advise you on the next step. Delivery timing can vary by route and local conditions.

    Is my quote request a confirmed booking?

    No. A quote request opens a draft email for you to review and send. Your shipment is not booked until Ocean Bridge confirms the service and booking details with you.
