Privacy notice

Last updated November 14, 2026

This notice describes how information is handled by the current Ocean Bridge tracking website. It should be reviewed and updated to reflect the final operating company, hosting, and applicable laws before public launch.

Information you provide

When you look up a shipment, the tracking ID is sent to the Ocean Bridge server to retrieve its tracking record. Quote and support forms prepare a message in Gmail with the details you enter. The message is not sent automatically; you can review or edit it before sending. Gmail is a separate service governed by Google's privacy terms.

Shipment and administration data

Shipment records are maintained by Ocean Bridge administrators for tracking and customer support. In this prototype, records are stored in a local JSON file on the server. Admin sign-in uses an essential session cookie to keep an authenticated session. Do not enter sensitive personal, payment, or identity information in shipment notes.

Cookies and analytics

The current site uses a session cookie only for the admin area. The public tracking page does not require an account. If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added later, this notice should be updated before they are enabled.

Retention and access

Shipment record access and removal are managed by authorized administrators. The current prototype does not define an automated retention schedule. Contact Ocean Bridge to ask about information associated with a shipment.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Ocean Bridge contact form and select the support option.
