Website terms

Last updated November 14, 2026

These terms describe use of the Ocean Bridge tracking website. They are a general website notice, not a shipping contract. Final terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel and aligned with the operating company's booking documents and service locations before public launch.

Tracking information

Tracking results show events currently recorded in the Ocean Bridge system. Updates may not appear immediately and are not a guarantee of a shipment's location, condition, or delivery time. An estimated delivery date, when shown, is an estimate and may change.

Quotes and bookings

Submitting a quote form prepares a Gmail draft for you to review and send. A quote request is not a booking, price offer, or confirmation of service. A booking is confirmed only when Ocean Bridge separately confirms the details with you.

Routes and customs

Service availability depends on the origin, destination, shipment contents, and operational requirements. Customs rules, duties, and clearance decisions are determined by the relevant authorities. Contact Ocean Bridge for route-specific guidance before shipping.

Acceptable use

Use the site lawfully and only for shipment information you are authorized to access. Do not interfere with the website, attempt to gain unauthorized access, or submit false or harmful content through its forms.

Availability and changes

The website may be updated or unavailable while being maintained. Ocean Bridge may revise these website terms as its services and systems change. Any terms for an actual shipment are governed by the documents supplied for that booking.

Contact

For questions about these website terms, use the Ocean Bridge contact form.
